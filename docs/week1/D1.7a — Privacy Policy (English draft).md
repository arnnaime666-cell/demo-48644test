# Week 1 — D1.7 Privacy Policy (English draft — for Client + counsel approval)

**Status:** Draft for `https://educateed.ca/privacy` (W1-S1.9 → S1.10). Publish only after Client + counsel approval.  
**Note:** This draft does **not** constitute legal advice (from `docs/recording-pipeda-launch-policy.md`).

## 1. Introduction

EducateED ("we", "us") operates `educateed.ca`, an Ontario-based K–12 one-to-one tutoring marketplace. We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with PIPEDA and applicable Canadian privacy laws.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account:** Profiles — id, email, full_name, phone, role (admin/tutor/student/parent), province, timezone, verification flags, children_info.
- **Tutor:** Bio, subjects, grade_levels, hourly_rate, session_duration, stripe_account, application/quality statuses, documents, ratings.
- **Sessions:** tutor_id, student_id, child_id, scheduled_at, timezone, status, pricing (cents), payout, dispute, recording consent, whiteboard.
- **Children:** parent_id, name, grade_level.

## 3. How we use it

To provide tutoring sessions, process payments via Stripe, schedule video via Daily.co, send email via Resend and OTP via Telnyx/Twilio, and to comply with law. We minimize collection and retain only as needed for service + legal holds.

## 4. Storage & residency

- **Supabase** (Postgres + Storage) — region `us-east-2` (US). 
- **Vercel** edge (CDN), **Stripe**, **Daily.co**, **Resend/Telnyx** — subject to their DPAs. 
- Full residency detail: `D1.6 — Data-Residency Report v1`.

## 5. Sharing

Stripe (payments), Daily.co (video), Resend (email), Telnyx/Twilio (SMS OTP). We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Retention

- Session data per PIPEDA retention policy; `financial_transactions` and `admin_logs` are append-only hash-chained.
- Whiteboards: 30 days (disputed held). Recordings: disabled at launch; future retention 30/60/90 only with both-party consent.

## 7. Your rights (PIPEDA)

Access, correction, withdrawal of consent, and deletion requests via `/admin/privacy` intake (data_deletion_requests). Contact: [to be added — SUPPORT_EMAIL].

## 8. Security

RLS on all 21 tables; privileged columns trigger-guarded; `documents` bucket private (signed-URL only); `avatars` public. Rate limiting enforced.
