## 1. Acceptance

By using `educateed.ca` you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

## 2. Accounts & roles

- Roles: admin, tutor, student, parent — each has separate access controls (see `lib/auth/guards.ts`).
- You are responsible for your account credentials and for information you provide.

## 3. Tutor engagement

- Tutors are independent. Application statuses: pending / approved / rejected / flagged. Rejected may reapply after 30 days.
- Tutor documents may be flagged for review before approval.
- Quality: good / flagged / warned / suspended / banned; rating threshold 3.5 triggers review.

## 4. Bookings, sessions & video

- Checkout: 30-min hold (live) → 10-min (proposal BKG-06) after migration.
- Join window: 5 min before to 60 min after end. Max 2 participants. Daily.co rooms are private.
- Session types: immediate vs scheduled; 15-min buffer enforced.

## 5. Pricing & payments (Stripe)

- Prices in CAD (cents). Invoices via `api/payments/invoice`.
- Checkout creates `payment_intent`; escrow held until completion + 48h dispute window; payout retry +1h ×3.
- Commission tiers: starter 20% / standard 18% / premium 15% — taken from customer charge, never added on top.

## 6. Cancellation, disputes & payouts

- Cancel: >24h full refund if paid; <24h no refund; requires reason.
- Dispute: only completed sessions, within 48h from `completed_at`, one per session, 6 categories.
- Payout: blocked by open dispute; tuttor Stripe Connect required; idempotency `session-payout-{id}`.

## 7. Recordings & whiteboards

- Recordings disabled at launch (PIPEDA safe). Future requires both-party consent, retention 30/60/90, deletion sweep.
- Whiteboards: 30-day retention; disputed held; batch 500.

## 8. Privacy

See Privacy Policy (`/privacy`). PIPEDA deletion intake: `POST /api/account/deletion-request` rate-limited.
